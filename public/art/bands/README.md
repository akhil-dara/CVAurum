# Header art bands

Four abstract bands a document can carry behind its header
(`theme.artBand`; None is the default and one click away in the header's
Style popover and in the Design panel). Each is generated abstract art at
1200x300, under 70 KB, and holds no text, no logo and no likeness, so the
words of a header are the only words on the page.

| File | Name | Palette |
|---|---|---|
| `navy-gold.webp` | Navy gold | midnight navy, slate grey, gold veining |
| `terracotta.webp` | Terracotta | warm cream ground, copper contour lines |
| `cobalt.webp` | Cobalt | cobalt blue, ink black, chalk white blocks |
| `emerald.webp` | Emerald | deep emerald folds on near-black |

The band is drawn behind the header at its full width, under a wash of the
page colour that keeps the header's words readable; the PDF export embeds
the same image, and the Word export prints it as a strip above the header.
